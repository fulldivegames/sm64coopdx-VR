PROPELLER MUSHROOM (PC VR AND QUEST)

Regular 60-second power-up. Available in the Spawn Menu and weighted box pool.
Power-Up Spawn/Enable has its own enable checkbox and 1-100 weight, default 50.
It replaces the previous special power-up; another pickup replaces it normally.
Uses regular special music, or alternate music when that option is selected.
Big Hands retains its separate 30-second default and optional 60-second timer.

Press Jump again during any normal airtime to burst upward once, including
tiny hops, water exits, slope jumps and lava rebounds. There is no minimum
jump height or upward speed. A fresh press/gesture is required.
With Physical Jumping enabled, another upward jump gesture also activates it.
Lower/rearm the same hand or alternate hands; a held first gesture cannot
automatically activate the burst. Touching down on ground/slopes, entering water,
or bouncing off lava recharges the burst, not the timer. Deaths, cutscenes and
underwater swimming are not airborne activation states.
Steer while rising and slowly gliding down. The VR camera does not spin.
Landing squarely on a normally stompable enemy (including while drilling)
relaunches you at the same strength as the manual burst, without refreshing
the power-up timer. Side hits, Bullies and breakable boxes do not relaunch you.

Hold the Crouch binding, or physically crouch with Physical Crouching enabled,
to descend fast. While held, the tornado's opacity is 100% and its spin is
three times normal. Its texture retains the gaps between the tornado strands.
Release crouch midair to brake back to the slow glide and transparent tornado.
This crouch drill only applies to the Propeller Mushroom, NOT ordinary twirling.

The in-game VR Tutorial > Special Moves includes the same controls.

The Wii-style red helmet and yellow rotor attach to the rendered head joint.
The helmet is hidden from the local first-person wearer, visible in third person.
Customize the Propeller preset in the normal player palette/color editor, then
export it as Propeller to save the power-up colors. Cap controls the helmet;
Emblem controls the rotor; Overalls/Shirt/Gloves/Shoes control the outfit.
Restore your usual player palette afterward: the Propeller preset is applied
only while the power-up is active. Existing Propeller.ini files are preserved.
With Grab Cap At Any Time enabled, grip + trigger near your head lets you inspect
the cosmetic power-up helmet in your hand, including in first person. Release
near your head to replace it, or release elsewhere to drop/throw a cosmetic copy.
This never removes the power-up or changes its timer. The Hammer Suit now has a
head-mounted helmet and torso-mounted back shell. Holding a cap/helmet blocks
hammer charging until it is released, preventing the shared controls conflicting.

Adapted from New Super Mario Bros. Wii's propeller ascent and spin descent;
the 60-second timer and VR input mapping are this mod's implementation.
