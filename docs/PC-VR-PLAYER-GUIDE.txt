SM64 CO-OP DX VR v0.9 - VISUALS, SPEEDRUNNING AND VR IMPROVEMENTS

Extract the complete ZIP into its own folder and launch SM64-Co-Op-DX-VR.exe.
Keep all included DLLs and resource folders beside the executable.
You must supply your own legally obtained Super Mario 64 US ROM.
The game can reuse your previously supplied ROM from its user-data folder
(normally AppData/Roaming/sm64coopdx), so updates do not require another copy.

Connect your headset using your OpenXR runtime. SteamVR supports Steam Link
and ALVR. Enable VR in the game's settings. Controller bindings are available
in VR settings; the default pause input is the left thumbstick click.

This update's main change is batched renderer uploads for large maps. It also
includes Big Hands in the special spawn pool, with extended hand interactions
and climbing. Performance gains depend on your map and hardware.

Optional normal maps can be enabled in VR display effects. They are disabled
by default. Special power-ups remain unavailable in Regular Public Lobbies.

Use the in-game update option for future PC VR releases. A separate upstream
coopdx_updater.exe is intentionally not included. Do not mix this package
with a regular flat-screen Co-op DX installation.

Your existing save data and settings are not included in this package.

CURRENT FEATURES
The in-game VR Tutorial covers Big Hands, speedrunning, visual options and caps.
Big Hands uses the enlarged hands for grabbing and physical punches. Hold Grip
at a surface and pull to climb; alternate hands between supported surfaces,
trees and hangables. Release Grip to detach. Special powers are disabled in
Regular Public Lobbies.

VR > Speedrunning contains timer setup, split names, size, position and color.
Choose Split Count and Confirm. Show Timer and Splits enables the viewer;
Reveal Splits as You Go adds the next row after each split. Assign Timer Start /
Split in Controller Bindings. See VR-SPEEDRUN.txt for PC/Quest setup imports.

Special > Filters includes Cell Shaded and the grayscale Super Mario Land
filter. Character Select uses a floating theater panel for its menu and preview.
Immersion includes crossed, stationary tree billboards. With Grab Cap at Any
Time enabled, hold Grip and Trigger near your head to remove the hat. Either
button can be pressed first. The life icon shows a red X when the hat is absent.

PC keyboard dictation runs locally using the included speech folder. Press Mic,
speak, then press Mic again to finish and transcribe; keep the keyboard open
while it processes. Closing the keyboard cancels. Set your headset microphone
as the default recording device. See speech/PC-DICTATION.txt for details.
